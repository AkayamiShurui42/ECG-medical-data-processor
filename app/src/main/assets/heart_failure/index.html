Interpretation and references

When SVI and HR are provided, cardiac index is estimated as SVI × HR ÷ 1000. When SVI and LV mass index are provided, myocardial contraction fraction is estimated as SVI ÷ (LVMI ÷ 1.05 g/mL) × 100. These estimates depend on method, load and measurement quality.

Preserved ejection fraction does not guarantee normal forward output; reduced stroke volume or small chambers alone do not prove heart failure. A normal natriuretic peptide should be interpreted with the other evidence, and alone does not exclude HFpEF.
